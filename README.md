# Milatery Factory

A compact browser game prototype where you search for futuristic military schematics, fabricate the weapon, stash it in your inventory, and challenge rotating enemy factions.

## Features

- **Search-to-build fabrication** – type part of a weapon name to surface matching schematics, then press **Build** (or Enter) to create it.
- **Inventory tracking** – completed weapons slide into your inventory while the UI tallies total damage output.
- **Mission control skirmish** – press **Engage Enemy** to enter a side-on arena where you pilot your freshly built weapon, dodge incoming fire, and overwhelm foes.
- **Dramatic defeats** – fall in battle and the cockpit erupts into a stylized crimson splash before the next rival faction steps in.
- **Soft 3D styling** – layered gradients, perspective transforms, and subtle animations provide a lightweight three-dimensional feel without heavy rendering.

## Getting started

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. No build tooling is required.

### Combat controls

- **W / A / S / D** (or arrow keys) to jump, strafe, and drop.
- **Space** to fire your equipped weapon. Firing rate scales with the damage of your crafted arsenal.
- **Esc** or **Leave Mission** to exit the engagement overlay at any time.
